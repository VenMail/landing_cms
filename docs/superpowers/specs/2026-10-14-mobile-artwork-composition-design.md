# Mobile Artwork Composition Design

## Goal

Make the mobile-apps illustration read as a visible human, on-the-go supporting element while retaining the real Venmail captures as the primary visual.

## Composition

- Keep the existing two authentic device captures and app-store destinations.
- Scale the secondary capture down and move both captures to the right at desktop widths.
- Keep the café/transit illustration visible in the left-bottom portion of the artwork area rather than placing it fully behind the devices.
- Preserve the mint and lilac shape treatment, transparent illustration asset, and current text.
- At mobile widths, stack the visual without clipping, horizontal overflow, or controls obscuring the captures.

## Verification

- The mobile-apps section renders the illustration and both real captures.
- Desktop and 390px screenshots visibly show the person/plant treatment.
- Store links and section order remain unchanged.
