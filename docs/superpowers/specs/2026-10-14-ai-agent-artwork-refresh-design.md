# AI inbox agents artwork refresh

## Goal

Replace the glossy blue 3D AI inbox image with an illustration that belongs with Venmail's landing-page artwork.

## Direction

Create two wide editorial variants for the AI inbox agents section:

1. Illustration-led: a flat, friendly agent motif framed by inbox, chat, and routine cards.
2. Product-led: layered charcoal product panels with a smaller flat agent motif.

Both variants use a warm off-white background, charcoal structure, coral as the primary accent, and pale mint and lilac as supporting colors. They avoid photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, vendor marks, embedded copy, and third-party logos.

## Selection criteria

Choose the variant that retains clear negative space for the section layout, reads at desktop and mobile crop sizes, and resembles the surrounding Venmail editorial/product artwork rather than a stock AI image.

## Integration

The selected raster asset replaces `public/images/ai-inbox-agents-teaser.webp` without changing the section's layout or copy. The discarded comparison asset remains outside the published asset path.

## Verification

- Inspect both generated variants against existing landing art.
- Confirm the selected asset has no legible accidental text or third-party marks.
- Run the landing production build after replacement.
